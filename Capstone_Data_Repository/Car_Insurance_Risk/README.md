# README

## Dataset description
This dataset captures information on auto insurance customers at the initial point of policy purchase ($t_0$). Its purpose is to enable analysis and predictive modeling to understand customers’ future behavior within the company. Primary uses could include:

- initial customer segmentation
- risk and propensity modeling (e.g., claim probability, coverage utilization, and retention)
- prioritizing commercial and service actions (onboarding and coverage education)
- constructing derived variables (features) for underwriting and pricing models

### Possible question
Does initial point of policy purchase reliably predict future customer coverage utilization? (Make sure to weight or otherwise account for length of coverage if you pursue this question, however.)
