# README

## Dataset description
During the initial stages of drug development, exposure to certain chemicals can lead to to acute liver injuries, or hepatoxicity, which poses a serious threat to human health. Achieving the precise safety profile of a compound is challenging due to complex and expensive testing procedures. These data provide an _in silico_ approach to aid in the identification of hepatoxic chemicals without risking human health during drug development. It can also mitigate this developmental cost. The dataset contains the chemical profiles of over 2500 chemicals and drugs with and without known induced liver damage (hepatoxicity). These data could be used to produce a a reliable predictive model that drug manufacturers could use to screen future candidates for hepaxtoxicity risk in the earliest stages of development, thereby preventing both possible harm and lost development costs.
